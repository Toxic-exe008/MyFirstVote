🗳️ Voter's Guide

Everything a first-time voter needs to know — how to vote, what to carry, what to expect, and why your vote matters.

🌟 First Time Voter?

Welcome! Voting for the first time is a milestone. Here's everything you need to be ready on election day.

📋

Check Your Name on the Electoral Roll

Visit voters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline App to verify your name is on the electoral roll. You need to be enrolled to vote.

🗺️

Find Your Polling Booth

Your polling booth is assigned based on your registered address. Check it on the ECI Voter Portal or SMS "EPIC<space>YourVoterID" to 1950.

🕖

Arrive on Time

Polling booths open at 7:00 AM and close at 6:00 PM. Arrive early to avoid queues. If you are in queue before 6 PM you will be allowed to vote.

🔏

Your Vote is Secret

Voting is done via an EVM (Electronic Voting Machine). No one can see who you voted for. Your choice is completely confidential.

🎒 What to Carry

You must carry a valid photo identity document. Any ONE of these is accepted by the ECI:

🪪

EPIC (Voter ID Card)

The Electors' Photo Identity Card is the primary document. If you have it, carry it.

📱

Aadhaar Card

Your Aadhaar card (physical or digital on DigiLocker / mAadhaar app) is accepted as valid ID.

🛂

Passport / Driving Licence

Your Indian passport or driving licence issued by any RTO is a valid alternative ID.

🏦

Other Accepted IDs

  • PAN Card
  • MNREGA Job Card
  • Smart Card issued by RGI
  • Bank / Post Office passbook with photo
  • Central / State Govt employee ID card
  • Health Insurance Smart Card (ESIC / RSBY)
  • Pension document with photo
✅ Dos & ❌ Don'ts on Voting Day

Follow these rules to ensure your vote counts and no issues arise at the polling station.

✅ What You SHOULD Do

  • Carry a valid photo ID document
  • Check your name and polling booth number before going
  • Arrive early and stand in queue patiently
  • Follow the instructions of polling officials
  • Press the button on the EVM firmly against your candidate's name/symbol
  • Wait for the beep from the EVM confirming your vote
  • Check the VVPAT slip to verify your vote was recorded correctly
  • Maintain silence inside the polling station
  • Exercise your vote independently without influence
  • Report any irregularity to the Presiding Officer or Election Commission helpline (1950)

❌ What You MUST NOT Do

  • Do NOT carry your mobile phone or camera inside the voting booth
  • Do NOT photograph or record the EVM screen or your vote
  • Do NOT take photos inside the polling station
  • Do NOT campaign or display party symbols within 200m of the booth
  • Do NOT accept money, liquor, or gifts in exchange for your vote
  • Do NOT vote more than once (it is a criminal offence)
  • Do NOT impersonate another voter
  • Do NOT create disturbance at the polling station
  • Do NOT enter the polling station armed or with any weapon
  • Do NOT reveal your vote to anyone — it is secret by law
🏛️ How India's Election System Works

As per the Election Commission of India (ECI) — a step-by-step guide to how democratic elections are conducted.

1

Announcement of Election Schedule

The Election Commission of India announces the election schedule and the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) comes into effect. The MCC prohibits political parties from announcing new schemes and bans certain campaign activities to ensure a level playing field.

2

Electoral Roll Revision & Voter Registration

The ECI updates the electoral rolls before every election. Citizens who turn 18 on or before January 1 of the election year can register via voters.eci.gov.in or Form 6 at their local BLO (Booth Level Officer). You must be a citizen of India and mentally sound to register.

3

Filing of Nominations

Candidates file their nomination papers along with a self-sworn affidavit disclosing assets, liabilities, educational qualifications, and criminal cases (if any). This affidavit is publicly available on MyNeta.info. After scrutiny, the final list of candidates is published.

4

Campaign Period

Candidates and parties campaign across constituencies. Campaigns must follow the Model Code of Conduct. Campaign spending is capped (₹28 lakh per candidate for Assembly elections in Maharashtra). Paid news, booth capturing, and voter inducement are prohibited. Campaign ends 48 hours before polling day (silent period).

5

Polling Day — Your Vote

On polling day, voters go to their assigned booth. Each voter's name is verified against the electoral roll. After identity verification, the voter's finger is marked with indelible ink and they are issued a ballot slip. The voter then presses the EVM button next to their chosen candidate's name. A VVPAT (Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail) slip flashes for 7 seconds to verify the vote.

6

Counting of Votes & Results

On counting day (usually 1–3 days after polling), EVMs are unsealed and votes are counted under tight security. The candidate with the highest number of votes in a constituency wins (First Past The Post system — FPTP). No minimum percentage is required to win. Losers are not given a share of seats.

7

Government Formation

For Maharashtra Assembly elections, the party or alliance that wins more than 145 out of 288 seats forms the government. The Governor invites the leader of that group to form the government. The leader is sworn in as Chief Minister. If no single party/alliance has majority, a hung assembly results in coalition negotiations.

8

Types of Elections in India

Lok Sabha (General Elections) — Every 5 years, 543 constituencies, elects Members of Parliament. PM is the leader of the majority party/coalition.  |  Vidhan Sabha (State Assembly) — Every 5 years per state (unless dissolved early), elects MLAs. CM is leader of majority.  |  Local Body Elections — BMC, Gram Panchayat, Municipal Councils — conducted by State Election Commission.  |  Rajya Sabha — Members elected by state legislators, not directly by voters.

⚖️ Important Rules to Remember

Key provisions under the Representation of the People Act, 1951 and ECI guidelines:

🔞

Age Requirement

You must be 18 years or older as on January 1 of the registration year to vote. To contest elections, the minimum age is 25 for Lok Sabha / Vidhan Sabha.

🖊️

Indelible Ink

Your left index finger is marked with indelible ink after voting. This prevents double voting. The ink stays for several days and cannot be washed off easily.

📵

No Phones in Voting Booth

Section 128 of the RPA 1951 mandates secrecy of voting. Photographing your vote or the EVM screen is a criminal offence punishable with up to 1 year in jail.

🧾

NOTA Option

None of the Above (NOTA) is available as a ballot option on all EVMs (the last option). You can use NOTA if you do not wish to vote for any candidate — but NOTA votes do not affect the winner.

♿

Accessibility & PwD Voters

Persons with Disabilities (PwD) and elderly voters above 85 years are entitled to Postal Ballot or home voting facility. Polling stations must have ramps, wheelchairs, and Braille EVMs.

🚨

Voter Helpline

Election Commission of India 24×7 helpline: 1950. Report booth capturing, inducement, or any violation to this number or the cVIGIL app.
